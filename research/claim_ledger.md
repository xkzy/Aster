## Review discipline

- Every quantitative claim in a final report must be traced to the paper’s table/figure, with exact benchmark/compiler/flags and an uncertainty or repetition description where available.
- Every formal guarantee must name the modeled language, theorem, assumptions, and whether the result is mechanized, paper proof, or informal argument.
- Search result snippets and third-party walkthroughs are leads only. If the paper cannot be retrieved, mark the claim provisional or omit it.
- No Aster experiment or novel algorithm result is recorded here.
